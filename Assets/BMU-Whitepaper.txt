British Material Unit (BMU) — Whitepaper

Our defence industry disposes of mass textile waste annually: graded-out smocks, parachutes and camouflage nets built to specifications far exceeding civilian clothing, commonly known as fast fashion. BMU treats this waste as a national asset, championing material sovereignty, transforming disposals from linear to a circular regenerative design model.

We are a designer-led unit. Designers are integral nodes in repositioning assets before being defined waste, and build Proof of Design (POD) to claim and have material access granted. We source, archive and remanufacture via the reverse engineering of garments.

Per operation we create design systems which secure textiles and their material intelligence found in each garment; uniting the intelligence between designers and garments to solve circulation of defence textiles.
