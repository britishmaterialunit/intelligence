Turkish Army 2000s M2008 Digital Camouflage Field Shirt

Nation: Turkish
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: TA2000M2008DCFS

Turkey's own digital pattern, issued from 2008 and unlike anything else in this archive: the shapes are tigerstripe, the rendering is pixel. Most armies went to digital by breaking up a woodland; the M2008 digitised a tigerstripe instead, and the result reads as neither.

The shirt is cut light, in a cotton blend ripstop, with the buttons hidden behind a placket so nothing shows on the front. Two chest pockets, a pouch on the sleeve, epaulettes at the shoulder and adjustable cuffs.

Measured flat, it runs 24" pit to pit and fits a regular large, with a 24" sleeve and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
