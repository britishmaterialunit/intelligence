Bulgarian Army 1960s Wool Water Bottle

BUA1960WWB

An aluminium water bottle in a brown wool felt cover, the kit a Bulgarian soldier drank from in the field. The cover is the point of it: wool holds water and water evaporating off it keeps what is inside cool, which is a flask that works without anything in it working.

It is cut as one piece and laced down one side through punched eyelets, drawn in at the neck by a cord so the bottle sits inside it rather than slides through. The screw cap is held to the collar by a chain so it cannot be put down and lost, and a sprung steel hook at the neck clips it to a belt or a harness.

Dated by the holding it came out of rather than by a label.
