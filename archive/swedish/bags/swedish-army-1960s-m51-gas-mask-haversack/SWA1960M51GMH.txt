Swedish Army 1960s M51 Gas Mask Haversack

Nation: Swedish
Type: Bags
Archive ref: SWA1960M51GMH

The Swedish Army 1960s M51 Gas Mask Haversack is cut from 100% cotton canvas.

It was made to carry the Swedish M51 gas mask with its filters and kit, and takes its unusual shape from them — the bag is cut around its contents rather than to a rectangle. Inside are clip pockets and pouches of varying shape and size, again cut to the components they were meant to hold. It closes with three clips and a canvas strap, and the shoulder strap adjusts and unhooks.

Bags of this pattern were made in quantity through the early Cold War, first as army issue and later for civilian distribution against chemical attack.
