Belgian Army 1960s M64 Olive Green Field Jacket

Nation: Belgian
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: BEA1960M64OGFJ

The Belgian Army 1960s M64 Olive Green Field Jacket is cut from wind resistant 100% cotton canvas.

It has 4 torso button pockets & 2 rear "poacher's" pockets, totalling 6 pockets, button & zipper closure, secreted hood, drawstring waist & hem, Velcro adjustable cuffs, unisex design, reinforced elbow pads, Velcro ID strip, Belgian flag patch, solid classic olive green colour, shoulder epaulettes and armpit vents.

Measured flat, it runs chest size: 44" | suggested size: medium regular, sleeve length: 21.5" / X short and collar to hem: 28".

Vintage Militaria / Dated 1989. Genuine Belgian Defence Forces / Belgian Armed Forces Surplus.
