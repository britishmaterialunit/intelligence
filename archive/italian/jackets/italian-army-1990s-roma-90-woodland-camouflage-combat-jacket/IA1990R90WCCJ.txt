Italian Army 1990s Roma 90 Woodland Camouflage Combat Jacket

Nation: Italian
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: IA1990R90WCCJ

The Italian Army Roma 90 combat jacket was made during the 1990s, this example dated 2002.

It features two internal pockets and one pocket per sleeve, a hidden button closure, reinforced elbow pads, button adjustable cuffs, ventilated armpits and Velcro ID and rank patches, cut heavy duty in 100% cotton. The Roma 90 woodland sits close to the American M81 in colour but draws its shapes differently.

Measured flat, it runs 37" along the chest and fits a regular small figure with sleeve lengths of 24.5" and a collar to hem of 25 inches.
