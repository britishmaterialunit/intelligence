Italian Army 1990s Roma 90 Woodland Camouflage Combat Jacket

Nation: Italian
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: IA1990R90WCCJ

The Italian Army 1990s Roma 90 Woodland Camouflage Combat Jacket is cut from 100% cotton.

It carries similar colourway to US M81 pattern and Italian Army Roma 90 Woodland camouflage pattern.

It has 2 internal pockets & 1 pocket per sleeve, hidden button closure, reinforced elbow pads, Velcro ID & rank patches, button adjustable cuffs, ventilated armpits and heavy duty & hard wearing.

Measured flat, it runs chest size: 37" | suggested size: small regular, sleeve length: 24.5" / regular and collar to hem: 25".
