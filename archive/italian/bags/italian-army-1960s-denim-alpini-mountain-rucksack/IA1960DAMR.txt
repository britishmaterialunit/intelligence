Italian Army 1960s Denim Alpini Mountain Rucksack

Nation: Italian
Type: Bags
Archive ref: IA1960DAMR

The Italian Army Alpini rucksack is a 1960s pattern, this example dated 1992, and it was made for the mountain troops.

It features one large main compartment with side pouches and three smaller pouches, a drawstring neck and adjustable straps, cut in a durable cotton canvas in a denim blue you would not expect from an army.

It measures roughly 19" by 16", which is about 30 litres of carry.
