Dutch Army 1990s SPE Olive Green Chest Rig

Nation: Dutch
Type: Webbing
Archive ref: NLA1990SOGCR

The Dutch Army SPE chest rig is a 1990s pattern issued from the 2000s onwards, made by S.P.E.

It features a clasp release buckle, adjustable waist and shoulders, a zipper closure pocket and a MOLLE field for adding to it, cut in a heavy duty nylon webbing in solid olive green.

Measured flat, it runs 46" across, which puts it between a medium and a large.
