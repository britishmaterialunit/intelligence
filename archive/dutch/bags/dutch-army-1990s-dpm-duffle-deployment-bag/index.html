Dutch Army 1990s DPM Duffle Deployment Bag

NLA1990DDDB

The bag a Dutch soldier's kit moved in, in the Netherlands' own woodland DPM. It is a deployment bag rather than a pack: it carries everything for a posting and is not meant to be marched in.

It loads through a full-length zip down the top, with grab handles either side and a webbing shoulder strap long enough to sling across the back. The whole of it is one weight of cotton canvas in the pattern, strap webbing included.
