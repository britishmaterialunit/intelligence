Dutch Army 1990s DPM Shirt

Nation: Dutch
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: NLA1990DS

A short sleeved field shirt printed in Dutch Woodland DPM, issued through the 1990s to be worn under the M93 combat jacket or on its own in warm weather.

Plain construction throughout — a ribbed crew neck in solid olive green with a matching neck tape inside, set-in short sleeves, no pockets and no fastenings. The camouflage runs across the body and sleeves with no attempt to match the print at the seams, as issued. The example photographed has faded and softened with wear, the browns and greens gone slightly chalky against the sand.

Measurements, label and date to be taken from the garment in hand.
