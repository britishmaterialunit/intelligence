Dutch Army 1990s M93 DPM Woodland Camouflage Jacket

Nation: Dutch
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: NLA1990M93DWCJ

The Dutch Army M93 jacket was made during the 1990s, this example dated 1990.

It features two zipper chest pockets, internal pockets and a sleeve pocket, a button closure, adjustable cuffs, shoulder epaulettes, Velcro patch fields and an internal drawstring waistband and hem, cut in a 65% cotton 35% polyester blend and printed in Dutch Woodland DPM. Heavy duty, with the Dutch flag at the sleeve.

Measured flat, it runs 23.5" pit to pit and fits a slim large figure — the label suggests 100 to 105cm and a height of 160 to 180cm — with sleeve lengths of 23.5" and a collar to hem of 29.5 inches.
