Dutch Army 1990s M93 DPM Woodland Camouflage Combat Jacket

Nation: Dutch
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: NLA1990M93DWCCJ

The Dutch Army M93 combat jacket was made during the 1990s, this example dated 1994.

It features four torso pockets and a sleeve pocket, a hidden popper button and zipper closure, adjustable cuffs, shoulder epaulettes, Velcro patch fields and an internal drawstring waistband and hem, cut heavy duty in a 50% cotton 50% polyester blend and printed in the Dutch take on Woodland DPM. The Dutch flag is still on the sleeve.

Measured flat, it runs 43" along the chest and fits a regular small figure — the label suggests 85 to 90cm and a height of 160 to 180cm — with sleeve lengths of 24.5" and a collar to hem of 37 inches.
