Romanian Army 2010s Tan Thermal Ripstop Coat

Nation: Romanian
Type: Coats
Archive ref: ROA2010TTRC

A cold weather coat of the Forțele Armate Române, issued through the 2010s, cut in a 95% cotton and 5% polyester ripstop and quilted with thermal lining for its full length.

It closes on a zip behind a Velcro storm flap, with a built hood and collar lined in fleece, two waist pockets, Velcro adjustable cuffs, an adjustable drawstring and shoulder epaulettes. Velcro patch fields sit at both chest. Solid desert tan throughout, with no pattern.

Measured flat it runs 21.5" pit to pit and fits a medium regular, with a short sleeve length of 22" and a collar to hem of 34".
