Romanian People's Army 1970s Khaki Wool Greatcoat

Nation: Romanian
Type: Greatcoats
Archive ref: ROPA1970KWG

The Romanian People's Army 1970s Khaki Wool Greatcoat is cut from 100% wool.

It has double breasted button closure, 2 flap waist pockets & interior pocket, engraved buttons, heavy duty & durable, partial cotton lining, Warsaw Pact / Eastern Bloc kit, hook and eye collar, shoulder epaulettes and solid khaki colour.

Measured flat, it runs pit to pit: 19" | suggested size: medium slim - label suggests a "36" chest which is a British 36" chest, sleeve length: 24.5" / regular and collar to hem: 49".

Vintage Militaria / Dated 1975 / Cold War Communist Era. Genuine Romanian People's Army / Armata Republicii Socialiste România. Surplus.
