Romanian Army 1990s M1990 Leaf Camouflage Jacket

Nation: Romanian
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: ROA1990M1990LCJ

A Forțele Armate Române field jacket in the M1990 woodland "leaf" pattern, in a polyester and cotton blend, issued from 1994 and through the 2000s.

Button closure down the front, two buttoned chest pockets, a buttoned pocket on each sleeve, shoulder epaulettes and adjustable wrist cuffs. The leaf print runs green and near-black over a pale ground, darker and heavier than the long sleeve shirt issued in the same pattern.

Measured flat it runs 23" pit to pit and fits a large slim, with a long sleeve length of 27" and a collar to hem of 34.5".
