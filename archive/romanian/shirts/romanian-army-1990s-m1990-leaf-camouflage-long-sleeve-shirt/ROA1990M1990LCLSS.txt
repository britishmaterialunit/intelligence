Romanian Army 1990s M1990 Leaf Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt

Nation: Romanian
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: ROA1990M1990LCLSS

The Romanian Army M1990 shirt was issued between 1990 and 1994, the first pattern of the post-revolution army.

It features a button closure, two button chest pockets, shoulder epaulettes, adjustable wrist cuffs and an interior strap for holding them rolled, cut in cotton. The M1990 leaf is Romania's own woodland, and it is becoming scarce.

Measured flat, it runs 46" along the chest and fits a regular large figure — the label states "41 I" — with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 32 inches.
