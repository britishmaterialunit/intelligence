Romanian Army 1990s M1994 Vine Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt

Nation: Romanian
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: ROA1990M1994VCLSS

The Romanian Army 1990s M1994 Vine Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt is cut from cotton.

It carries the M1994 "vine" camouflage, a short-lived pattern issued as the country came out of the turmoil of the 1989 revolution and dropped again after some four years.

It has button closure, 2 button chest pockets, shoulder epaulettes, adjustable wrist cuffs and an interior strap for rolled-up cuffs.

Measured flat, it runs chest size: 42" / medium regular - label states "38 I", sleeve length: 24.5" / regular and collar to hem: 29".

Vintage Militaria / issued 1994 - 2000 / dated 1998. Genuine Romanian Army / Forțele Armate Române Surplus.
