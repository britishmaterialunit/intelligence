Romanian Army 1990s M1994 Vine Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt

Nation: Romanian
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: ROA1990M1994VCLSS

The Romanian Army M1994 shirt was issued from 1994 to 2000, this example dated 1998, and the vine was a short lived pattern — brought in as the country came out of the turmoil of the 1989 revolution and dropped again after some four years.

It features a button closure, two button chest pockets, shoulder epaulettes, adjustable wrist cuffs and an interior strap for holding them rolled, cut in cotton.

Measured flat, it runs 42" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label states "38 I" — with sleeve lengths of 24.5" and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
