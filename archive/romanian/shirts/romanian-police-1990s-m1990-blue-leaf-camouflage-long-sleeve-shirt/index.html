Romanian Police 1990s M1990 Blue Leaf Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt

ROP1990M1990BLCLSS

The Romanian Police shirt was issued between 1994 and 2002, this example dated 2002.

It features a button closure, two button chest pockets, shoulder epaulettes, adjustable wrist cuffs and an interior strap for holding them rolled, cut in a polyester and cotton blend. The pattern is the Army's M1990 leaf taken into blue for the Ministry of the Interior — the same drawing, a different service.

Measured flat, it runs 22" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure with sleeve lengths of 24.5" and a collar to hem of 31 inches.
