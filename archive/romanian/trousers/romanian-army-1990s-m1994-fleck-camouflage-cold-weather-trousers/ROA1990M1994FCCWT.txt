Romanian Army 1990s M1994 Fleck Camouflage Cold Weather Trousers

Nation: Romanian
Type: Trousers
Archive ref: ROA1990M1994FCCWT

Cold weather trousers in the M1994 woodland "fleck" pattern, dated 1994, cut in a durable cotton and lined throughout with thermal wool.

Six pockets — two at the waist, two tool pockets and two at the rear — over a button fly, with 2" belt loops, elasticated cuffs and boot straps at the hem. The fleck print is particular to Romania: soft green and brown blotches broken up by small near-black flecks, closer to a German Flecktarn in method than to the leaf pattern issued alongside it.

Waistband measures 18.5" flat and fits a medium regular, with an inside leg of 28.5" and a total leg length of 41".
