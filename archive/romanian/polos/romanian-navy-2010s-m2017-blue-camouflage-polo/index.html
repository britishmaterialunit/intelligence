Romanian Navy 2010s M2017 Blue Camouflage Polo

RON2010M2017BCP

The Romanian Navy M2017 polo has been issued from 2017 onwards.

It features a quarter button closure, a classic polo collar, one chest pocket, shoulder epaulettes and elasticated cuffs, cut in 100% cotton. The same fractal M2017 the Army wears, taken into Navy blue — camouflage for a ship, which is to say camouflage for nothing at all.

Measured flat, it runs 22" pit to pit and fits a slim large figure with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 26 inches.
