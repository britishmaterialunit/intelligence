Romanian Army 2010s M2017 Woodland Camouflage Polo

Nation: Romanian
Type: Polos
Archive ref: ROA2010M2017WCP

The Romanian Army M2017 polo has been issued from 2017 onwards.

It features a quarter button closure, a classic polo collar, one chest pocket, shoulder epaulettes and elasticated cuffs, cut in 100% cotton. The M2017 is a fractal print in a Multicam colourway — Romania arriving at the same answer as everyone else, by its own route.

Measured flat, it runs 18" pit to pit and fits a regular small figure with sleeve lengths of 23" and a collar to hem of 25.5 inches.
