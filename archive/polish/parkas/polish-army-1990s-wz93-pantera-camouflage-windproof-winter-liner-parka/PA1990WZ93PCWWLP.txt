Polish Army 1990s WZ93 Pantera Camouflage Windproof Winter Liner Parka

Nation: Polish
Type: Parkas

The Polish Army WZ93 winter parka is a 1990s pattern, most of them dated in the 2000s.

It features seven pockets — two chest, two large waist and three on the sleeves — a hidden button front, button adjustable cuffs and shoulder straps, cut in a windproof and showerproof heavy cotton canvas over a detachable full length quilted liner. Pantera is Poland's own camouflage and looks like nobody else's. Polish flag patches still on it.

Measured flat, it runs 46" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label suggests around 92cm and a height of 172cm — with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 32.5 inches.
