Czechoslovak Army 1980s Orange Pyjama Shirt

Nation: Czechoslovak
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: CSA1980OPS

The Czechoslovak Army pyjama shirt was made during the 1980s, this example dated 1986.

It features a button closure, long sleeves and a regular fit, cut in a comfortable cotton in a cheerful orange and chocolate that nobody would guess was army issue. Warsaw Pact kit, and the most unlikely thing in the archive — whatever else the Eastern Bloc was short of, it was not colour.

Measured flat, it runs 47" along the chest and fits a regular large figure with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 28.5 inches.
