Czechoslovak Army 1960s M60 Vz63 Cold Weather Thermal Trouser Liner

Nation: Czechoslovak
Type: Liners
Archive ref: CSA1960M60VZ63CWTTL

The Czechoslovak Army Vz63 trouser liner is a 1960s piece, Warsaw Pact cold weather kit of the plainest sort.

It features a thermal lining and was made to button inside issued trousers rather than to be worn on its own — the half of a winter uniform nobody photographs.

No measurements were taken for this one when it came in, so it is filed on the garment alone.
