Czechoslovak Army 1980s M85 Field Olive Green with Hood Parka

Nation: Czechoslovak
Type: Parkas
Archive ref: CSA1980M85FOGWHP

The Czechoslovak Army M85 field parka was made during the 1980s, this example dated 1987.

It features two button chest pockets and two slit hip pockets, a hidden button and zipper closure, a drawstring waistband, a button detachable hood and shoulder epaulettes, cut in a durable cotton canvas in classic olive drab. It can take a cold weather liner and collar, neither included — which means without them there is room to size up.

Measured flat, it runs 22.5" pit to pit and fits a slim medium figure — the label suggests around 94cm — with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 31 inches.
