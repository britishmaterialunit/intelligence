French Police Municipale 2000s Codura Bulletproof Vest

Nation: French
Type: Vests
Archive ref: FRPM2000CBV

A Police Municipale body armour carrier in navy Cordura, worn over the uniform shirt.

Shoulder straps and a wide waist belt, both in navy elastic webbing and both closing on hook and loop, so the carrier draws in to the wearer rather than hanging off them. A full width loop field runs across the lower back for the belt to land on. Shown here from the back, plain and unmarked — the identification panels sit on the front.

Photographed empty of its panels. Measurements, label and protection rating to be taken from the carrier in hand.
