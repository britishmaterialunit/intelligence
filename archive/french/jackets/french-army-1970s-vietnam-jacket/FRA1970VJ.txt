French Army 1970s Vietnam Jacket

Nation: French
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: FRA1970VJ

A short cotton field jacket in faded olive drab, cut blouson-style to sit at the waist.

Full length brass front zip, a single flapped chest pocket on the right closed with a button, and a slash pocket on the left carrying a strip of loop tape for a name or unit patch. A second loop panel sits above the right chest. Buttoned epaulettes at the shoulder, a stand collar, elbow reinforcement panels, and buttoned tabs at both the cuffs and the waistband. The cotton has softened and faded unevenly, with the olive gone grey across the sleeves.

Labelled size S. Measurements and date to be taken from the garment in hand.
