French Army 1990s CCE Camouflage F2 Jacket

FRA1990CCF2J

The French Army F2 jacket was issued through the 1990s.

It features two zipper chest pockets, a button closure, reinforced elbow pads, Velcro strips and shoulder epaulettes, cut hard wearing in 100% cotton and printed in CCE — Camouflage Central Europe, the French woodland. The F2 was the French combat jacket for two decades and it looks like nothing else.

Measured flat, it runs 22.5" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 28 inches.
