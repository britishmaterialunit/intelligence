Slovak Air Force 2000s Thermal Flight Jacket

Nation: Slovak
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: SKAF2000TFJ

The Slovak Air Force thermal flight jacket was made in the early 2000s, and it is close kin to the British aircrew Ballyclare jackets.

It features a zipper and stud button closure, one zipper chest pocket, two slit hip pockets and internal pockets, an elasticated waistband and adjustable cuffs, cut in a viscose and cotton blend with built in thermal quilting and made flame resistant. Solid olive green, with the "SLOVENSKO" sleeve patch and Slovak flag still on it. Scarce kit — Slovakia is a small air force.

Measured flat, it runs 27" pit to pit and fits a regular extra large figure with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 28 inches.
