Slovak Army 1990s M97 Camouflage M65 Jacket

Nation: Slovak
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: SKA1990M97CM65J

The Slovak Army M97 jacket is a 1990s pattern, this example dated 2005, and it is the M65 shape rendered in Slovak cloth.

It features seven pockets — a chest pocket, two waist, a shoulder pocket and three at the rear — a button closure, a hood secreted in the collar, Velcro ID strips and adjustable cuffs, shoulder loops and armpit vents, cut in a durable cotton and polyester. The M97 woodland sits close to the American M81, with the Slovak Army embroidery at the sleeve.

Measured flat, it runs 53" along the chest and fits a slim large figure — the label suggests around 100cm and a height of 172cm — with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 33.5 inches.
