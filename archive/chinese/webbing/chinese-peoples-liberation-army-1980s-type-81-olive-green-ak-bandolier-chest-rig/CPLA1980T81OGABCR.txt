Chinese People's Liberation Army 1980s Type 81 Olive Green AK Bandolier Chest Rig

Nation: Chinese
Type: Webbing
Archive ref: CPLA1980T81OGABCR

The Chinese People's Liberation Army Type 81 chest rig was made during the 1980s, and this one is new old deadstock — never issued, never worn.

It features seven pouches in all, four small and three larger, with adjustable shoulder and waist straps, cut in a durable cotton canvas in solid olive green. ChiCom kit of the kind carried from the Vietnam era onwards, built to hold AK magazines and little else.

Measured flat, it runs 58.5" across its entire width.
