British Army 1990s Woodland DPM Camouflage Body Armour Cover Vest

Nation: British
Type: Armour Covers
Archive ref: BA1990WDCBACV

The British Army body armour cover was issued from the 1990s, this one printed in the iconic Woodland DPM rather than the desert print of its neighbour on the shelf.

It features a front pouch and a rear pouch with a Velcro adjustable waist. The armour itself is not included — this is the cover alone.

No measurements were taken for this one when it came in, so it is filed on the garment alone.
