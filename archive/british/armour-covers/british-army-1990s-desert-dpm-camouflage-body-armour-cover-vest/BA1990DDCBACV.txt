British Army 1990s Desert DPM Camouflage Body Armour Cover Vest

Nation: British
Type: Armour Covers
Archive ref: BA1990DDCBACV

The British Army Desert DPM body armour cover was issued from the 1990s, this example from the early 2000s.

It features one front pouch and one rear pouch, a button and Velcro closure, a rank tab and a Velcro adjustable waist, cut in a durable 67% polyester 33% cotton blend and printed in classic British Desert DPM. The armour itself is not included — this is the cover alone, carrying a good deal of patching by whoever had it before.

The label suggests a height around 190cm and a chest around 120cm, which puts it at a regular extra large.
