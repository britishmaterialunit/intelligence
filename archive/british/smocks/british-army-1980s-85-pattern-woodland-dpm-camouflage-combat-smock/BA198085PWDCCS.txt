British Army 1980s 85 Pattern Woodland DPM Camouflage Combat Smock

Nation: British
Type: Smocks
Archive ref: BA198085PWDCCS

The British Army 1980s 85 Pattern Woodland DPM Camouflage Combat Smock is cut from windproof heavy cotton.

It carries 1985 pattern / P85 series combat set and vibrant 85 pattern DPM Woodland camouflage colourway.

It has 4 large button pockets, 1 sleeve pocket 1 internal fishtail pouch, 4 internal rear pouches & 1 internal chest pouch, zipper & button front closure, adjustable cuffs, shoulder epaulettes, soft & comfortable material, interior torso & waist drawstrings, falklands and heavy duty & hard wearing.

Measured flat, it runs pit to pit: 22" | suggested size: medium slim - label recommends a chest around 96cm, sleeve length: 24" / short - label recommends a height around 180cm and collar to hem: 31".

Vintage Militaria / Issued 1990 - 1994. Genuine British Army Surplus.
