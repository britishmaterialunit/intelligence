British Army 2010s MTP Camouflage Windproof Smock

BA2010MCWS

The British Army MTP windproof smock has been standard issue from 2011 onwards.

It features ten pockets — two zipper chest, four button torso, two sleeve and two hand warmers — a zipper and Velcro closure, adjustable Velcro cuffs, a wire framed drawstring hood, a drawstring hem, reinforced elbow pads and zipper armpit vents, cut in a durable shower resistant cloth printed in Multi Terrain Pattern. Windproof, and the current smock the British Army actually wears.

Measured flat, it runs 46" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label suggests around 96cm — with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 34 inches.
