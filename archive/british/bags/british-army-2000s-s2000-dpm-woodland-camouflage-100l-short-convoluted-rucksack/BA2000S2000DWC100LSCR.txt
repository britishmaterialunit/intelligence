British Army 2000s S2000 DPM Woodland Camouflage 100L Short Convoluted Rucksack

Nation: British
Type: Bags
Archive ref: BA2000S2000DWC100LSCR

The short convoluted Bergen of the S2000 system, dated 2003, in woodland DPM. A hundred litres in the main sack with side pouches that come off, and the convoluted back that gives the pattern its name — shaped to sit against a loaded webbing belt rather than flat against the spine.

Water repellent nylon, and IRR treated: the dye is chosen so the bag reads the same as the terrain under night vision as well as in daylight.

Shoulder straps and waist adjust, the compartments draw closed inside and clasp shut outside, and the whole thing is made to be overloaded.
