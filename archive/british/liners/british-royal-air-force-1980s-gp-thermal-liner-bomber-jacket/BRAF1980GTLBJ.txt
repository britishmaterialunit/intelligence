British Royal Air Force 1980s GP Thermal Liner Bomber Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Liners

The British Royal Air Force 1980s GP Thermal Liner Bomber Jacket is cut from wool & polyester blend.

It has 2 waist pockets, zipper closure, shoulder epaulettes, elasticated cuffs & waistband, senior aircraftsman's rank slides, detachable thermal liner, turnover storm collar and classic Air Force blue colour.

Measured flat, it runs pit to pit: 22" | suggested size: medium slim - label reads 96M suggesting a 96cm chest, sleeve length: 24.5" / regular and collar to hem: 24.5".
