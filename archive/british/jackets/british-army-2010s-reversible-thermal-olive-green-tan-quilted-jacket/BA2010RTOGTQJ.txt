British Army 2010s Reversible Thermal Olive Green Tan Quilted Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Jackets

A thermal layer that is two jackets depending on which way round it is worn: olive green on one face, tan on the other, quilted between a nylon shell and a polyester lining. Issued from 2010 and still in service.

It is built to go under something rather than over it — elasticated at the waist and cuffs so a shell closes cleanly over it, with two waist pockets and nothing on the outside to catch.

Measured flat, it runs 24" pit to pit and fits a regular medium, with a 25" sleeve and a collar to hem of 32 inches.
