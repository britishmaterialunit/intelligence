British Army 1990s S95 Woodland DPM Camouflage Ripstop Field Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Jackets

The British Army S95 ripstop field jacket was issued between 1995 and 2003.

It features four large button torso pockets and two zipper chest pockets, a zipper and Velcro closure, adjustable Velcro cuffs and waist and hem drawstrings, cut heavy duty in durable ripstop and printed in the iconic British Woodland DPM. It runs large, so there is room to size up.

Measured flat, it runs 51" along the chest and fits a slim large figure — the label states around 104cm — with sleeve lengths of 28" and a collar to hem of 37.5 inches.
