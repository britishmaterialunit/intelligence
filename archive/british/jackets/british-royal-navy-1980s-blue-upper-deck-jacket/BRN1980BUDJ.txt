British Royal Navy 1980s Blue Upper Deck Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: BRN1980BUDJ

The British Royal Navy upper deck jacket is an 1980s pattern made by Mullion, this example dated 2023.

It features two waist pockets, a chest pocket, a sleeve pocket and internal pockets, a zipper and Velcro closure, a hood secreted in the collar, rubberised internal collar and cuffs and Velcro adjustable cuffs, cut in 100% polyester with a waterproof polyurethane coating over a flame resistant cotton lining. Water and wind resistant, heavy duty, and built for the deck it is named after. Solid blue grey.

Measured flat, it runs 26.5" pit to pit and fits a slim extra large figure — the label reads XL, a chest of 112 to 120cm and a waist of 100 to 108cm — with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 30 inches.
