British Army 1980s No.1 Royal Signals Dress Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: BA1980NRSDJ

The British Army No.1 dress jacket is an 1980s pattern, this one made up for the Royal Signals and dated 2019.

It features two chest and two waist pockets, a button closure with a hook and eye at the collar, engraved Royal Signals "staybrite" buttons, Royal Signals collar badges, belt loops, shoulder epaulettes and decorative cuff buttons, cut in 100% wool over an internal lining. Its sergeant wore it well: a gilt thread rank patch, a bullion thread marksman's qualification, and a ribbon bar carrying the Iraq and Afghanistan medals, the latter with rosette, alongside the Queen's Diamond Jubilee 2012 and the Army Long Service and Good Conduct. A whole career on one chest, in solid black.

Measured flat, it runs 21" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure — the label recommends 108cm although it looks to have been tailored down — with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
