British Army 1990s S95 Desert DPM Camouflage Combat Jacket

BA1990S95DDCCJ

The British Army S95 combat jacket in Desert DPM was issued through the 1990s, this example dated 1990 to 1994.

It features a button and zipper closure, two chest button pockets, a pen pocket and a sleeve pocket, adjustable button cuffs and a turnover storm collar, cut light in soft cotton and printed in classic British Desert DPM. Worn through the Gulf wars and UN peacekeeping operations, and some come with British flag patches still on the sleeve.

Measured flat, it runs 44" along the chest and fits a slim medium figure — the label suggests around 96cm — with sleeve lengths of 21.5" and a collar to hem of 27.5 inches.
