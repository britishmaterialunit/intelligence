British Army 2010s No.2 FAD Royal Regiment of Scotland Dress Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: BA2010NFRROSDJ

The British Army No.2 dress jacket in Future Army Dress is current issue from the 2010s, this one made up for the Royal Regiment of Scotland and dated 2016.

It features two button chest pockets, two flap hip pockets and three internal slit pockets, a button closure with engraved Royal Regiment of Scotland buttons, belt loops and shoulder epaulettes, cut in 100% wool in the solid "barracks brown" FAD adopted across the Army.

Measured flat, it runs 48" along the chest and fits a slim extra large figure — the label states 120cm with a waist of 112cm — with sleeve lengths of 27" and a collar to hem of 28 inches.
