British Royal Air Force 1990s Wet Weather Coat Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Coats
Archive ref: BRAF1990WWCJ

The British Royal Air Force 1990s Wet Weather Coat Jacket is cut from lightweight waterproof 100% polyamide.

It has 100% PA + PTFE membrane, zipper & Velcro closure, 2 flap hip pockets, slit waist pockets & 1 chest pocket, Velcro cuffs, adjustable drawstring waistband, can be fitted with a detachable thermal liner, (NOT included), double zipper, shoulder epaulettes, funnel neck, solid Air Force blue colour, *May or may not have a thin hi-vis strip on the back and (varies due to manufacturing year).

Modern Militaria / Issued 2010's Onwards. Genuine British Royal Air Force Surplus.
