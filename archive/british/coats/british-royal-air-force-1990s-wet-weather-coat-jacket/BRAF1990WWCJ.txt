British Royal Air Force 1990s Wet Weather Coat Jacket

Nation: British
Type: Coats
Archive ref: BRAF1990WWCJ

The British Royal Air Force wet weather coat is a 1990s pattern issued from the 2010s onwards.

It features a zipper and Velcro closure over a double zipper, two flap hip pockets, slit waist pockets and a chest pocket, Velcro cuffs, an adjustable drawstring waistband, a funnel neck and shoulder epaulettes, cut in a lightweight waterproof 100% polyamide with a PTFE membrane. It can take a detachable thermal liner, which is not included, and depending on the year it was made it may or may not carry a thin hi-vis strip across the back. Solid Air Force blue.
