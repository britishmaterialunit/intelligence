British Royal Air Force 1980s Raincoat Trench Coat

Nation: British
Type: Coats
Archive ref: BRAF1980RTC

The British Royal Air Force raincoat is an 1980s pattern, this example dated somewhere between 1994 and 2003, and the label inside reads "RAINCOAT, MANS, RAF, ALL RANKS" — which tells you most of what you need to know about how the RAF names things.

It features a double breasted button closure, two waist pockets with internal access, a full belt and shoulder epaulettes, cut in 100% gabardine wool that is wind and shower proof. A proper trench coat in classic Air Force blue, worn over the uniform rather than as part of it.

Measured flat, it runs 24" pit to pit and fits a slim large figure — the label suggests a chest around 108cm — with sleeve lengths of 25" and a collar to hem of 46 inches.
