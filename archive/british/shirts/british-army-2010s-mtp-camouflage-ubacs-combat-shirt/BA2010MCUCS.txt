British Army 2010s MTP Camouflage UBACS Combat Shirt

Nation: British
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: BA2010MCUCS

The British Army UBACS combat shirt has been issued from 2011 to the present day.

It features a pullover design with a quarter zip funnel neck collar, two Velcro sleeve pockets and button adjustable cuffs, cut in a 70% cotton 30% polyester blend. The body is plain olive green with the Multi Terrain Pattern kept to the sleeves — it is built to be worn under body armour, so there is no point printing what nobody will see. Unpadded.

Measured flat, it runs 41" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — its label is missing — with a collar to hem of 29 inches.
