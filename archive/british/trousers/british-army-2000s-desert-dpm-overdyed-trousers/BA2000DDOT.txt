British Army 2000s Desert DPM Overdyed Trousers

Nation: British
Type: Trousers
Archive ref: BA2000DDOT

The British Army Desert DPM trousers were issued through the 2000s, and this pair has since been put through a vat.

It features an elasticated waist with an internal drawcord, elasticated gathered ankle cuffs, a straight full cut leg and a plain front with no belt loops, cut light in cotton. The overdye has taken the four colour desert print down to a flat charcoal — the pattern is still legible in the cloth but the sand and stone have gone, so what is left is the shape of the brushstrokes rather than their colour. Desert DPM was Britain's arid theatre print from the first Gulf War until MTP replaced it, and the dye is a later hand: surplus made wearable as clothing rather than as uniform.
