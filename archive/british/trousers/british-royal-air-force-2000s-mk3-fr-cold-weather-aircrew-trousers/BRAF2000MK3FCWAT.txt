British Royal Air Force 2000s MK3 FR Cold Weather Aircrew Trousers

Nation: British
Type: Trousers
Archive ref: BRAF2000MK3FCWAT

The British Royal Air Force MK3 aircrew trousers are a 2000s pattern, this pair dated 1993.

It features a button and zipper closure, built in adjustable suspenders, a Velcro adjustable belt, various pockets and zipper calves for getting them on over boots, cut in a fire resistant outer shell with waterproofing over a Gore-Tex inner lining. Heavy duty cold weather kit in solid olive green, and properly made — everything about them is built for sitting still at altitude.

Measured flat, they run 17" across the waistband and fit a regular extra small figure — the label reads "SIZE 1" for a waist of 69 to 79cm and a height of 152 to 161cm — with an inside leg of 27" and a total length of 43 inches.
