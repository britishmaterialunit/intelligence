Spanish Army 2000s M09 Pixel Leaf Camouflage Ripstop Jacket

Nation: Spanish
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: SPA2000M09PLCRJ

The Spanish Army M09 jacket is a 2000s pattern, this example dated 2013.

It features a button closure, two chest and two sleeve pockets, an internal waist adjuster, adjustable cuffs, Velcro patch fields and shoulder epaulettes, cut in a durable ripstop of 73% cotton, 25% polyamide and 2% elastane. The M09 is Spain's pixel leaf — a digital print that still reads as leaves. Spanish flag at the sleeve.

Measured flat, it runs 19.5" pit to pit and fits a slim medium figure with sleeve lengths of 23.5" and a collar to hem of 27.5 inches.
