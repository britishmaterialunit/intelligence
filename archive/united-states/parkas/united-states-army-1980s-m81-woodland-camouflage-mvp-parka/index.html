United States Army 1980s M81 Woodland Camouflage MVP Parka

USA1980M81WCMP

The United States Army MVP parka is an 1980s pattern, this example dated 1996, and the label reads "PARKA, COLD WEATHER, CAMOUFLAGE".

It features two large waist pockets, a zipper and stud button closure, Velcro adjustable cuffs, a drawstring waistband and hem, a hood and reinforced elbow pads, cut water resistant in 100% nylon and printed in M81 Woodland.

Measured flat, it runs 46" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label states "MEDIUM - REGULAR" — with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 30 inches.
