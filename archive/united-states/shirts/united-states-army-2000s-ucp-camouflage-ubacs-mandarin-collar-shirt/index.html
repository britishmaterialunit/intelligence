United States Army 2000s UCP Camouflage UBACS Mandarin Collar Shirt

USA2000UCUMCS

The United States Army UBACS is a 2000s pattern, this example dated 2005, printed in UCP — the Universal Camouflage Pattern, the digital grey-green the Army spent a fortune on and then quietly withdrew.

It features two chest pockets and a pocket on either sleeve, a zipper and Velcro front closure, a Velcro mandarin collar, Velcro ID strips, Velcro adjustable cuffs and reinforced elbow pads that can take further padding, cut wind resistant in a durable 50% cotton 50% nylon ripstop. Iraq War issue, worn under armour.

Measured flat, it runs 42" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure — the label states SMALL, a chest of 33 to 37 inches, and LONG for a height of 71 to 75 inches — with sleeve lengths of 25" and a collar to hem of 28.5 inches.
