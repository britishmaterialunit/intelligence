The United States Marine Corps BDU was made through the 1980s and 1990s, and the label inside reads "COAT, WOODLAND CAMOUFLAGE PATTERN, COMBAT" — the Americans name a thing by describing it.

Measured flat, it runs 45" along the chest and fits a regular large figure with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
