United States Marine Corps 1980s M81 Woodland Camouflage BDU Combat Jacket

USMC1980M81WCBCJ

The United States Marine Corps BDU was made through the 1980s and 1990s, and the label inside reads "COAT, WOODLAND CAMOUFLAGE PATTERN, COMBAT" — the Americans name a thing by describing it.

It features two chest pockets and two hip pockets, a button closure, button adjustable cuffs and reinforced elbow pads, cut in a 50% nylon 50% cotton blend and printed in the M81 Woodland that defined two decades of American soldiering. Marine Corps stamped.

Measured flat, it runs 45" along the chest and fits a regular large figure with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
