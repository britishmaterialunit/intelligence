United States Air Force 2010s Multicam Camouflage Combat Jacket

Nation: United States
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: USAF2010MCCJ

The United States Air Force combat jacket is a 2010s pattern, this example dated 2017, printed in Multicam.

It features two large chest pockets and two sleeve pockets, a zipper and Velcro closure, button adjustable cuffs and reinforced elbows, cut in a durable 50% cotton 50% nylon ripstop. McKnight's, and a medic: the U.S. Air Force tape, the medical corps patch and both an airman first class and a staff sergeant rank patch are still on it.

Measured flat, it runs 40" along the chest and fits a slim medium figure — the label reads "33 - X SHORT" — with sleeve lengths of 23" and a collar to hem of 24 inches.
