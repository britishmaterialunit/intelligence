United States Air Force 1990s Blue Utility Bomber Jacket

USAF1990BUBJ

A United States Air Force utility jacket in solid navy, labelled "JACKET, UTILITY, UNISEX", dated 1999.

It is cut in a 65% polyester 35% cotton blend, with a zipper closure, two waist pockets and an internal pocket, and elasticated cuffs and hem.

It measures 25" pit to pit, 27" in the sleeve and 29.5" from collar to hem — a large slim, the label stating "MEDIUM / XLONG".
