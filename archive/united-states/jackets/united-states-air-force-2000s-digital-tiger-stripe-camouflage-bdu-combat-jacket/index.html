United States Air Force 2000s Digital Tiger Stripe Camouflage BDU Combat Jacket

USAF2000DTSCBCJ

The United States Air Force BDU is a 2000s pattern, this example dated 2007, printed in the Air Force's own Digital Tiger Stripe.

It features eight pockets — four button pockets, two waist, two large chest and a sleeve pen pouch — a button closure, button adjustable cuffs and reinforced elbow pads, cut heavy duty in 50% cotton 50% nylon. Staff Sergeant Sanderson's, by the patches, and it went to Afghanistan.

Measured flat, it runs 48" along the chest and fits a slim extra large figure — the label reads "46 R" — with sleeve lengths of 27" and a collar to hem of 31 inches.
