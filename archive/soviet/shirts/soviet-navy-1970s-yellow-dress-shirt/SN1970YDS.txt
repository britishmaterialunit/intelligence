Soviet Navy 1970s Yellow Dress Shirt

Nation: Soviet
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: SN1970YDS

The Soviet Navy dress shirt is a 1970s pattern, this example issued 1986.

It features a button closure, two chest pockets, an adjustable button waist and button cuffs, cut in cotton in a solid yellow you would not expect from the Red Fleet. This one belonged to a "michman" — a warrant officer — and the rank boards are still on the shoulders.

Measured flat, it runs 41" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label states "48" — with sleeve lengths of 24" and a collar to hem of 25.5 inches.
