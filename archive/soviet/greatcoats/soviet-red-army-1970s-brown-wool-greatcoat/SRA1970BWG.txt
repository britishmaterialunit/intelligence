Soviet Red Army 1970s Brown Wool Greatcoat

The Soviet Red Army 1970s Brown Wool Greatcoat is cut from 100% wool.

Measured flat, it runs pit to pit: 20.5" | suggested size: medium slim, sleeve length: 24" / short and collar to hem: 48".
