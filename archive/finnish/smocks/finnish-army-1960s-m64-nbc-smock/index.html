Finnish Army 1960s M64 NBC Smock

FA1960M64NS

The Finnish Army nuclear biological chemical (NBC) smock was manufactured during the 1960s by Nokia of all people.

It features a double button closure, elasticated cuffs, drawstring hood & waist, a sleek monochrome silver outer and jersey like inner with the charcoal lining having worn off. Used for training and fallout operations, it's a one of a kind piece although there's a few knocking about still.

Measured flat, it runs 53" along the chest and fits a slim extra large figure with sleeve lengths of 25.5" and a collar to hem of 31 inches.
