Czech Army 1990s Cold Weather Liner Jacket

Nation: Czech
Type: Liners
Archive ref: CZA1990CWLJ

The Czech Army cold weather liner is a 1990s piece, this example dated 1990.

It features a button in design, a synthetic faux fur body with sateen sleeves and armpit vents, in a brown colour scheme. Warsaw Pact kit meant to button inside a Czech parka rather than be worn alone — if you want it in a jacket of your own it will need reworking. Thermal, and far more comfortable than it has any need to be.

Measured flat, it runs 23.5" pit to pit and fits a regular large figure with sleeve lengths of 22" and a collar to hem of 32 inches.
