Iraqi Army 1980s Desert DPM Camouflage Combat Jacket

Nation: Iraqi
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: IQA1980DDCCJ

The Iraqi Army combat jacket was issued from the late 1980s to 1990, which places it either side of the invasion of Kuwait.

It features a button and zipper closure, two chest button pockets, a pen pocket and a sleeve pocket, adjustable button cuffs and shoulder epaulettes, cut light in soft cotton. The camouflage is Iraq's own reading of Desert DPM — the same brushstroke idea as the British print but earthier, browner, a different desert entirely.

Measured flat, it runs 22" pit to pit and fits a slim medium figure — the label suggests around 96cm and a height of 170cm — with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 27 inches.
