Greek Army 1960s M65 Faux Fur Liner Jacket

GRA1960M65FFLJ

The Greek Army M65 liner is a 1960s piece, this example dated 1974, and it was made to button inside the Greek M65 rather than to be seen.

It features a faux fur lining under a silk like shell with a drawstring closure, in olive green. Warm, comfortable and far nicer than a liner needs to be — which is why soldiers wore them on their own as a smoking jacket.

Measured flat, it runs 37" along the chest and fits a regular small figure with sleeve lengths of 21.5" and a collar to hem of 27 inches.
