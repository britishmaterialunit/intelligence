Greek Army 1960s M65 Olive Green Jacket

Nation: Greek
Type: Jackets
Archive ref: GRA1960M65OGJ

The Greek Army M65 jacket was manufactured from the 1960s, this example dated 1967.

It features two chest and two waist pockets, a button closure, a drawstring closure, adjustable cuffs and shoulder epaulettes, cut in 100% cotton canvas in olive green. Greece's own take on the M65, and it can take a thermal liner, which is not included.

Measured flat, it runs 21.5" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 29 inches.
