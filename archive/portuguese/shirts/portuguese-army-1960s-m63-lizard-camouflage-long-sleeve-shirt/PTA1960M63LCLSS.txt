Portuguese Army 1960s M63 Lizard Camouflage Long Sleeve Shirt

Nation: Portuguese
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: PTA1960M63LCLSS

The Portuguese Army M63 shirt is a 1960s pattern issued into the 1980s, and it carries the lizard — Portugal's own version of the vertical French print, and one of the great camouflages.

It features a button closure, two button chest pockets, shoulder epaulettes and button cuffs, cut in cotton. Plain construction; the pattern is the piece.

Measured flat, it runs 18" pit to pit and fits a slim small figure with sleeve lengths of 22.5" and a collar to hem of 28.5 inches.
