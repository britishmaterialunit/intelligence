West German Army 1980s Olive Drab Parka With Thermal Liner

Nation: West German
Type: Parkas
Archive ref: WGA1980ODPWTL

The West German Army cold weather parka was issued to the Heer through the 1980s, this one dated 1982, in a durable cotton canvas.

It features five flap button pockets — two at the waist, two at the chest and one inside — a button and zip closure, drawstrings at the waistline and hem, adjustable wrist cuffs, a drawstring hood and shoulder epaulettes, with a detachable full length synthetic fur liner buttoned into the shell. Windproof and water resistant, in the classic olive drab. The liner is the whole point of it: the shell alone is a three season coat, and with the fur in it the thing is built for a German winter on exercise.

Measured flat, it runs 23" from pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure — the label reads Gr 5, a chest of 90 to 95cm — with a sleeve length of 24" and a collar to hem of 32 inches.
