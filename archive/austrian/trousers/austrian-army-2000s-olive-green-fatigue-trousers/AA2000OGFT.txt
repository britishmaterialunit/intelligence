Austrian Army 2000s Olive Green Fatigue Trousers

Nation: Austrian
Type: Trousers
Archive ref: AA2000OGFT

The Austrian Army olive green fatigue trousers are the Bundesheer's working dress leg, this example dated 2001. A heavy duty 50% polyester and 50% cotton blend in classic olive green.

They close with a button fly, carry two leg and two hip button pockets, and run a 1" belt loop around the waistband.

Measured flat, the waistband runs 17.5" and fits a medium slim figure — the label suggests a waist of 96 to 100cm, 37 to 39", though the cut is tailored smaller than that — with an inside leg of 32" and a total length of 43 inches.
