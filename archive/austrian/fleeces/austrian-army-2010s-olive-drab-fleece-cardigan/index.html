Austrian Army 2010s Olive Drab Fleece Cardigan

AA2010ODFC

The Austrian Army olive drab fleece cardigan is current issue with the Bundesheer, this example dated 2017. A 100% polyester fleece in classic olive drab, cut for cold weather.

It closes with a zip and carries two waist pockets and one internal, with thumb holes at the cuffs, an elasticated hem, an adjustable collar, reinforced elbows and shoulders, and velcro patches.

Measured flat, it runs 44" along the chest and fits a small regular figure — the label states 88 to 92cm — with sleeve lengths of 27" and a collar to hem of 30 inches.
