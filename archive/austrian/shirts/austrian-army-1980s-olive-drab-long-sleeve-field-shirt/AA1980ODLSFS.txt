Austrian Army 1980s Olive Drab Long Sleeve Field Shirt

Nation: Austrian
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: AA1980ODLSFS

The Austrian Army olive drab long sleeve field shirt is a 1980s pattern still on issue with the Bundesheer, this example dated 1990.

It features a button closure, two chest pockets, adjustable button cuffs and the same oversized buttons the jackets carry, with the Austrian federal eagle on the sleeve. A soft polyester and viscose blend in classic olive drab, worn as the layer under everything else.

Measured flat, it runs 45" along the chest and fits a regular medium figure — the label states 96 to 100cm — with sleeve lengths of 23.5" and a collar to hem of 28.5 inches.
