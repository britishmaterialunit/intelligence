Austrian Army 2000s Olive Drab Anzug 03 Alpine Jacket

AA2000ODA03AJ

The Austrian Army Anzug 03 alpine jacket was manufactured during the 2000s, this example dated 2008.

It features two zipper pockets, a sleeve pocket and internal pockets, a button and zipper closure behind a button wind flap, interior torso and waist drawstrings and button adjustable cuffs, cut in a polyester and cotton blend that is heavy duty and hard wearing. Built for the Alpine troops in solid olive drab, and generous enough that it sizes up easily.

Measured flat, it runs 25" pit to pit and fits a slim medium figure — the label states 88 to 92cm — with sleeve lengths of 26" and a collar to hem of 31 inches.
