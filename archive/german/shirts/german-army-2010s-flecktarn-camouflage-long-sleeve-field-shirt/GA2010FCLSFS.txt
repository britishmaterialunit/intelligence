German Army 2010s Flecktarn Camouflage Long Sleeve Field Shirt

Nation: German
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: GA2010FCLSFS

The German Army Flecktarn field shirt has been issued from the 2000s to the present day, this one dated 2011, in a soft cotton and polyester blend.

It features two popper button chest pockets and a sleeve pocket, a zip and popper button closure, adjustable Velcro cuffs, shoulder epaulettes, a Velcro identification strip and German flag patches at the sleeve. It is cut light, to be worn as the layer against the weather rather than under it, and carries the same five colour Flecktarn the parkas do.

Measured flat, it runs 40" along the chest and fits a slim medium figure — the label reads GrNr 1, a chest of 90 to 95cm — with a sleeve length of 24" and a collar to hem of 28 inches.
