The Serbian Army M93 parka is a 1990s pattern, this example dated 2000.

It features eight pockets — four button pockets, two "poacher" zipper pockets at the back waist, an interior pocket and one on the left arm — a button and zipper closure, adjustable cuffs, an elasticated waistband, hem and waist drawstrings, shoulder epaulettes, a detachable hood and a detachable cold weather liner, cut in a durable cotton blend. The M93 oak leaf is the pattern Yugoslavia broke up into.

Measured flat, it runs 23" pit to pit and fits a regular medium figure — the label suggests around 100cm and a height of 172cm — with sleeve lengths of 24.5" and a collar to hem of 35 inches.
