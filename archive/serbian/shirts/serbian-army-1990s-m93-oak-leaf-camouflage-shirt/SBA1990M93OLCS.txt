Serbian Army 1990s M93 Oak Leaf Camouflage Shirt

Nation: Serbian
Type: Shirts
Archive ref: SBA1990M93OLCS

The Serbian Army M93 oak leaf camouflage shirt is a durable cotton field shirt, this example dated 1998 and labelled by Yumco of Vranje. It carries the Yugoslav M93 oak leaf pattern the Serbian Army took on.

It features a button closure, two button pockets, adjustable cuffs and shoulder epaulettes.

Measured flat, it runs 25" pit to pit, 63cm, and fits a large regular figure — the label recommends a chest around 116cm, 45.5" — with sleeve lengths of 25", 64cm, for a height around 188cm, and a collar to hem of 28 inches, 72cm.
