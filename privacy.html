Privacy Policy

This notice explains what British Material Unit (“BMU”, “we”) does with personal data collected through britishmaterialunit.com. We are the data controller. You can reach us at hello@britishmaterialunit.com or Unit U, Wrexham Road, SS15 6PX, England, United Kingdom.

1. What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join or request early access.
  • What you send us — the content of an enquiry or archive request, including any garment reference and quantity.
  • Nothing else by default. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics.

2. Why, and on what basis

  • To reply to you, and to fulfil a request — our legitimate interest in responding, or performance of a contract.
  • To send occasional updates about BMU, where you asked for them — consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

3. Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered by a third-party form service, which handles the message in transit and passes it to our inbox. Our email is hosted by a third-party provider. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers log requests, including IP addresses, for security and operation. These providers act on our instructions or as controllers of their own infrastructure logs; we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Data may be processed outside the UK by the providers above, protected by the safeguards their terms provide. We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with it and for our own records, and mailing-list addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, or ask for it in portable form. Write to hello@britishmaterialunit.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer you may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

6. Cookies and local storage

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. Some pages remember small preferences — such as garments you have bookmarked in the archive — in your own browser’s local storage. That stays on your device, is not sent to us, and clearing your site data removes it.

7. Changes

If this notice changes, the revised version appears here with a new date above.

BMU sources, archives, remanufactures and circulates surplus defence textiles. The site publishes an archive of garments and materials, describes our operations, and lets you register interest or request material.

Dates and attributions are often inferred from markings and construction rather than documentation, and may be corrected without notice. Nothing in the archive is a warranty of origin, age, specification or condition.

3. Availability and requests

  • Listings are a record of what has passed through the archive, not a live stock position.

  • A request placed through the site is an invitation to treat. No contract exists until we confirm it in writing.
  • Quantities, lengths and prices are confirmed per operation, in writing.

4. Condition of goods

Goods are ex-service surplus, sold as seen. Wear, repair, fading, unit markings and previous alteration are inherent characteristics of the material, not defects. Where a garment is graded, the grade is our own assessment. Material intended for remanufacture is not sold as serviceable protective equipment, and must not be relied on as such.
